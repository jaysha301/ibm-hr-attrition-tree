# IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition sample

File: `WA_Fn-UseC_-HR-Employee-Attrition.csv` (1,470 rows, 35 columns).

This is IBM's fictional HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset, originally published by IBM data scientists and redistributed on Kaggle by Pavan Subhash as `pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset` under the **CC0 1.0 Universal** public-domain dedication.

Kaggle downloads require an account, so this copy was taken from the public GitHub mirror:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nelson-wu/employee-attrition-ml/master/WA_Fn-UseC_-HR-Employee-Attrition.csv

A UTF-8 byte-order mark was removed. The rows were not edited. It is a synthetic workforce sample, not records of real employees.
