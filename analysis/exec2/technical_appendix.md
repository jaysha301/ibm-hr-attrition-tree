## 1. Why this analysis, and the rules
The CEO viewed the app and asked for deeper drilling: stopping at the overtime split leaves 72% of staff (the no-overtime branch) undrilled, and a 1.5x rate floor can filter out larger groups at a lower rate ratio whose total effect on company attrition is bigger. Quinn confirmed the rule below as final. The standing requirement: keep drilling into large groups until the meaningful patterns are found.

**Amendment note in `thresholds.json`:** Amended at 2026-10-09 15:15:07 PDT (before any final run). Quinn's five gates are unchanged. Added, as reported-not-gating items: (1) a stability sensitivity with unpruned refits, because a first smoke test showed that 1-SE pruning sends about 3 in 4 refits inside the no-overtime branch to the root, which caps any group's stability share well below 50%; (2) the parent-branch view (gates against the parent branch rate) next to the company-average rule; (3) a safety cap of 6 defining conditions on drilling. Drilling every group that passes the parent-branch gates (before stability) cascaded to hundreds of thousands of groups, so groups chosen for drilling only by those gates are drilled while they have a single defining condition inside their branch. Final edit at 2026-10-09 15:28:43 PDT: made the sensitivity-refit settings and the storage floor explicit parameters; no rule changed.

| Gate | Rule (all on the full 1,470 rows unless stated) |
|---|---|
| 1 Size | n >= 100 and leavers >= 24 |
| 2 Impact | excess leavers = leavers - n x 0.1612 >= 15 (about 1 point of company attrition; points = excess / 1470) |
| 3 Rate | lift (rate / 0.1612) >= 1.25 AND lower end of the 95% Wilson interval > 0.1612 |
| 4 Held-out | at least 30 test employees, and test rate > test-set rate 0.1610 (71/441). Fewer than 30: 'supported on the full data, test too small', not cleared |
| 5 Stability | across 500 bootstrap refits fit on the group's own branch rows (overtime rows for overtime groups, no-overtime rows for no-overtime groups; minbucket 100, 10-fold CV, 1-SE pruning, depth up to 10), every defining variable is a primary split in > 50% of refits at a similar cut (within +/-0.10 of the share of the branch below the cut; categorical: same variable). Groups defined on all staff use the old rule: nodes 1-3 of depth-3 refits. |

Reported but not gating: the 1.5x lift (`lift_ge_1.5`), the parent-branch view (rate, lift, excess and impact against overtime 30.5% or no-overtime 10.4%; `pv_gates` applies gates 1-4 with the parent branch as the comparison and `pass_pv` adds stability), the old company-wide stability (`stab_old`: every defining variable including OverTime a primary split at nodes 1-3 of depth-3 company-wide refits, any cut), and a looser stability (`stab_unpruned`: unpruned depth-3 refits, see section 5). Impact in points = excess / 1,470 x 100. Impact 'if the group fell to its parent branch rate' = leavers - n x parent rate, an illustration, not a forecast.

| all staff | pruned | 3 of 500 | 99% |

| overtime | unpruned | 0 of 500 | 100% |
| no overtime | unpruned | 0 of 500 | 100% |

**Property of the rule, not of any group.** Inside the no-overtime branch, 1-SE pruning leaves 371 of 500 refits with no split, so no no-overtime group can exceed 26% stability by rule 5. ## 6. Drill and stopping rule
77 nodes were drilled (`drill_log.md`, `drill_log.csv`). For each node with 100+ people, every single split of every predictor was tested (cuts from the training rows inside the node), all five rules applied to each child, and any child that qualified (or passed the sensitivity checks, or was a one-condition group clear against its own branch) was drilled in turn. **Stopping rule: no remaining node with 100 or more people yields a child that qualifies.** Result: 2 qualifying group(s); 0 not drilled; safety cap of 6 conditions reached 0 time(s).
Deeper leaves with fewer than about 120 people have fewer than 30 test-set people and land in 'supported on the full data, test too small'.

## 11. Caveats
- **Fictional dataset** (IBM teaching data); nothing here describes a real workforce.
- **Nothing is causal**; groups describe who left more, not why.
- **Pay, job level, tenure and total experience are one correlated cluster** (experience also tracks age). They are shown as one career-stage picture; impacts are never added across them.
- **Age, gender and marital status** are descriptive only; groups defined by them are tested, counted and never headlined.
- **Snapshot timing:** tenure and survey fields are measured at the same time as the outcome.
- **Multiple comparisons:** thousands of overlapping groups were tested; the held-out and stability checks and the shuffle check are the guards, and the shuffle counts above show how far they go.
- **Stability is optimistic** (bootstrap duplicates in cross-validation), and for the no-overtime branch rule 5 is limited by how often 1-SE pruning keeps any split.

**Null checks that bear on group 2 and on the no-overtime branch.**

- Against the parent branch, gates 1-4 pass in a mean of 21.0 groups per within-overtime shuffle (95th percentile 74, maximum 126) and 62.0 per across-everyone shuffle (95th percentile 195); with stability, 0 of 200 within-overtime shuffles and 0 of 200 across-everyone shuffles produce any. In the real data 3,783 group(s) pass gates 1-4 against their branch (most are company-wide groups, judged against the company average, which is the same test as rules 1-4) and 1 also pass stability (group 2).
- Report this as: none of 200 shuffles produced a group that clears all five rules, and none produced a group that clears the parent-branch view with stability. This is a count, not a p-value. It supports 'not chance' for an income-within-overtime pattern, not for the exact $3,221 cut.
- The shuffled searches tested fewer groups than the real search (mean 9,252 across everyone and 6,875 within overtime, against 17,584 real), because drilling follows signal, which makes the shuffled search somewhat conservative against the real one. They also used 100 bootstrap refits per scope for stability, not 500.
- Gates 1-4 do not discriminate inside overtime: within-overtime shuffles pass a mean of 673 groups (95th percentile 753), because any large group inside overtime beats the company rate by construction. Rule 5 and the parent-branch view carry group 2.
- In the no-overtime branch the shuffle check has no power at rule 5: at most 26% of strict refits keep any split there, so 'no group passed' says nothing about whether a pattern exists. **Absence of a qualifying pattern is not proof of no pattern.** In plain words: in the non-overtime branch no stable single split is found, because pruned trees find no split in 371 of 500 refits, while career stage shows up as a family when trees are left unpruned (89% of unpruned refits split on at least one career-stage measure).
- The held-out check tests direction, not independence: size, impact, Wilson and stability use all 1,470 rows, which include the held-out rows, so the held-out result is a check that the direction holds, not a fully independent test.
- Group 2's held-out result (32 people, about 19 leavers, 59%) is imprecise: a 95% range for a rate on that many people runs from about 42% to 74%.
